    References¶

    The source behind each model. Equation and section numbers in the code refer to these editions.

    Radar fundamentals and detection¶

    1. M. I. Skolnik (ed.), Radar Handbook, 3rd ed., McGraw-Hill, 2008. ISBN 978-0-07-148547-0.
    2. M. A. Richards, Fundamentals of Radar Signal Processing, McGraw-Hill, 2005. ISBN 978-0-07-144474-3.
    3. D. K. Barton, Radar System Analysis and Modeling, Artech House, 2005. ISBN 978-1-58053-681-3.
    4. F. E. Nathanson, Radar Design Principles, 2nd ed., SciTech, 1999. ISBN 978-1-891121-09-8.
    5. B. R. Mahafza, Radar Systems Analysis and Design Using MATLAB, 3rd ed., CRC Press, 2013.
    6. J. V. DiFranco and W. L. Rubin, Radar Detection, Prentice-Hall, 1968.
    7. P. Swerling, "Probability of Detection for Fluctuating Targets," IRE Trans. Information Theory, 6(2), 269–308, 1960. doi:10.1109/TIT.1960.1057561
    8. W. J. Albersheim, "A Closed-Form Approximation to Robertson's Detection Characteristics," Proc. IEEE, 69(7), 839, 1981. doi:10.1109/PROC.1981.12025
    9. P. P. Gandhi and S. A. Kassam, "Analysis of CFAR Processors in Nonhomogeneous Background," IEEE Trans. AES, 24(4), 427–445, 1988. doi:10.1109/7.7185
    10. H. Rohling, "Radar CFAR Thresholding in Clutter and Multiple Target Situations," IEEE Trans. AES, 19(4), 608–621, 1983. doi:10.1109/TAES.1983.309350
    11. E. B. Wilson, "Probable Inference, the Law of Succession, and Statistical Inference," J. American Statistical Association, 22(158), 209–212, 1927.

    Propagation and scattering¶

    1. ITU-R P.676-13, Attenuation by atmospheric gases and related effects, 2022.
    2. ITU-R P.838-3, Specific attenuation model for rain for use in prediction methods, 2005.
    3. ITU-R P.839-4, Rain height model for prediction methods, 2013.
    4. J. S. Marshall and W. McK. Palmer, "The Distribution of Raindrops with Size," J. Meteorology, 5(4), 165–166, 1948.
    5. Y. Oh, K. Sarabandi and F. T. Ulaby, "An Empirical Model and an Inversion Technique for Radar Scattering from Bare Soil Surfaces," IEEE Trans. Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 30(2), 370–381, 1992. doi:10.1109/36.134086
    6. V. Gregers-Hansen and R. Mital, An Improved Empirical Model for Radar Sea Clutter Reflectivity, NRL/MR/5310--12-9346, Naval Research Laboratory, 2012.

    Electronic attack¶

    1. D. C. Schleher, Electronic Warfare in the Information Age, Artech House, 1999. ISBN 978-0-89006-526-0.
    2. F. Neri, Introduction to Electronic Defense Systems, 2nd ed., SciTech, 2006.
    3. R. Price, "A Useful Theorem for Nonlinear Devices Having Gaussian Inputs," IRE Trans. Information Theory, 4(2), 69–72, 1958.

    Tracking and estimation¶

    1. Y. Bar-Shalom, X. R. Li and T. Kirubarajan, Estimation with Applications to Tracking and Navigation, Wiley, 2001. ISBN 978-0-471-41655-5.
    2. S. S. Blackman, Multiple-Target Tracking with Radar Applications, Artech House, 1986.
    3. H. W. Kuhn, "The Hungarian Method for the Assignment Problem," Naval Research Logistics Quarterly, 2, 83–97, 1955.
    4. S. M. Sherman and D. K. Barton, Monopulse Principles and Techniques, 2nd ed., Artech House, 2011.
    5. S. J. Julier and J. K. Uhlmann, "A Non-divergent Estimation Algorithm in the Presence of Unknown Correlations," Proc. American Control Conference, 2369–2373, 1997.

    Imaging¶

    1. I. G. Cumming and F. H. Wong, Digital Processing of Synthetic Aperture Radar Data, Artech House, 2005.
    2. C. C. Chen and H. C. Andrews, "Target-Motion-Induced Radar Imaging," IEEE Trans. AES, 16(1), 2–14, 1980.

    1. IEEE Std 686-2017, IEEE Standard for Radar Definitions.
    2. NATO APP-6, Joint Military Symbology (affiliation shapes and colours on the scopes).
